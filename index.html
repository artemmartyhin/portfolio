2026 · Rust · open source

balq

Local, verified history of any contract's storage on a plain full node. No archive node, no indexer schema. It indexes a contract back to its deploy, follows the head and verifies every block it stores, using EIP‑7928 block‑level access lists.

2025–26 · exchange

Prediction markets exchange

Off‑chain matching engine with on‑chain settlement of EIP‑712 signed orders. Binary outcome tokens are minted and merged against USDC collateral; fills settle in batches through an exchange contract. Per‑user vault contracts come from a factory on first deposit. Market resolution runs as a Chainlink CRE workflow that writes verdicts to an on‑chain settler. Indexer tracks fills, positions and vault state from chain events.

Ordinals bridge

Bridge back end for Ordinals, BRC‑20 and Runes. zero knowledge

ZK wallet

ERC‑4337 account whose signature validation is a ZK proof: the client proves knowledge of the credential in a Groth16 circuit, the on‑chain verifier checks it in validateUserOp, nullifiers stop replay, and recovery is another proof instead of a guardian multisig. Bundler and paymaster integration for gasless flows.
